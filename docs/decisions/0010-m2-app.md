# 0010: M2 screens: the morning, Go to sleep and the System tab

Date: 2026-11-27
Status: accepted

## Context

The last M2 slice puts the System in front of the players: the morning they wake to, a way to end the day early, and somewhere to see what they've become.

## Decisions

**The morning lives at the top of Today.** It shows the player's System messages in a framed "System window" (the series' bracketed lines, in a serif), then the house's scene. It stays until the next night closes, so the morning is the first thing you see whenever you open the app that day. Past mornings, with both players' messages, are on the System tab.

**"Go to sleep"** sits at the bottom of Today, behind a confirm. Afterwards Today says you're asleep and hides Done, Tiny, Undo and grace, since the database refuses them anyway.

**A new System tab** is the character sheet:

- the class (the evolved name once chosen) and level
- an XP bar toward the next level, as of the last night
- every Skill with its name, tier and what it does
- the Level 10 Skill and evolution choices, with the one reroll
- past mornings

While you're asleep, choices are shown but locked.

**Grace days** appear as a "Use [Skill]" button on a habit that isn't done, is scheduled today, and (for a weekly goal) isn't already met, when a grace Skill has a charge left this week. The button records the habit in tonight's choices, and the engine decides at the close.

**Screens reload** when a tab comes into view and when the app returns to the foreground, so a morning that arrived while the app was open shows up.

**Arrive gets a "Use a different email" link**, so a session whose account no longer exists can't trap the player on a screen that can't save.

## Checked in the Simulator

Using seeded local data and `pnpm night:local`:

- the morning on Today
- the System tab: class, XP bar, Skills, mornings for both players
- logging and then "Go to sleep": logs lock, the night is processed, and the morning waits for the housemate
- picking a Level 10 Skill and evolution: the choices are saved and marked

The pass fixed three bugs: grace was offered on a weekly goal already met, a Skill's summary showed twice when the System hadn't written flavor, and screens didn't notice new data.
