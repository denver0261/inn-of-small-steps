# 0013: M3 screens: outings, the house and its orders

Date: 2026-11-27
Status: accepted

## Decisions

**Outings get their own screen**, reached from an "Around Liscor" card on Today. The card shows the stamina left today, or that an outing is waiting. The screen plays three events, one choice each. After each choice it shows the roll ("Rolled 14 + 3 against 10"), the outcome and what was found, then a summary of today's outings. Choices are saved as they're made, so an outing can be left and resumed. Under each choice the screen says its theme, and whether one of your Skills helps.

**The preview is the payout.** The screen replays today's outings with the same engine the nightly job uses (`previewOutings`), carrying bad-luck protection from one outing to the next. What it shows is what lands in the stash that night. A job test and a Simulator run confirmed it.

**The House tab** gains:

- the house scene: each built upgrade's line, then how each real room looks from its chores
- the four currencies, as of this morning
- tonight's orders, with cancel
- the ladder, with "Build tonight", or "Try tonight" when tonight's income might cover it
- the cookfire or kitchen recipes, with mastery
- the stash, with "Eat tonight" for food

The garden only offers itself once the rungs below it are built. "Invite your partner" hides once someone has joined.

## Checked in the Simulator

With seeded data:

- the outing card and two full outings, including a rare find and the last event's outcome
- the House tab: scene with a dusty kitchen, currencies, ladder, cookfire, stash
- ordering a meal

Then both players slept and `pnpm night:local` ran: both outings paid exactly what the screen showed, the stew was eaten (+5 stamina, 28 − 20 + 5 = 13), and the morning mentioned the claw.

The pass fixed three bugs: the last event's outcome was skipped, the garden offered to build out of order, and the invite button showed with a full house.
