# 0016: M4 screens: the door, quests, the Inn and recruits

Date: 2026-11-27
Status: accepted

## Decisions

The House tab gains four sections, above the upgrades:

- **At the door.** Each waiting visitor shows who they are, what they say, the quest in plain words, how long it runs (from tomorrow), the reward, and how long they'll wait. The buttons are Accept, Not now, and Serve a guest platter (when there's one in the stash). An answer shows who gave it and can be cancelled until the day closes.
- **Quests.** Each running quest shows its ask, whose it is, and its progress so far ("2 of 3 so far, until …"). The progress is computed in the app from the house's logs with the same engine function the nightly job uses (`questProgress`). Tonight's close may still finish it.
- **The Wandering Inn.** "Spend the evening up the hill" orders the evening and shows a short scene at once. The scene is the same all day for a player. It notes that someone from the Inn may come by tomorrow.
- **Helping out.** The recruits who stayed on, and what they do.

## Checked in the Simulator, then through a real night

With a seeded house (two visitors at the door, a running quest, a recruit), I accepted the landlord's quest and spent the evening at the Inn. After both players slept, `pnpm night:local` ran the night:

- the landlord's quest started
- the Worker's nightly supplies landed
- goodwill rose from 7 to 8
- an Inn regular and a rare student from Wistram arrived for the morning
- the morning's scene mentioned the Inn and both arrivals

The quest card's "0 of 3" was right: it's a solo quest, and only the housemate had eaten well.

The pass fixed one bug: the accepted note said "you said yes" in lowercase.

The game now has every milestone through M4: habits, the System, the house, and visitors. Next is the two-week playtest (decision 0006). It needs a hosted Supabase project, a Claude API key for real mornings, and the app on both phones.
